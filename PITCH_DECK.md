## 🎯 Top Judge Q&As (Defend with Confidence)

### Q1: "What if there is no internet in rural agricultural fields during an emergency?"
**Answer:**  
*"That is precisely why we engineered the **Offline Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Mesh Beacon** and **Single SMS / Missed-Call Fallback**. When SOS is triggered, the phone broadcasts a BLE beacon within 100 meters without internet. Any passing smartphone running the Saathi app or volunteer node receives the alert. Simultaneously, a compact cellular tower SMS transmits the landmark to the base station."*

### Q2: "How does the system know the woman is in distress if she cannot unlock her phone?"
**Answer:**  
*"We implemented 3 hands-free detection vectors:*  
*1. **Multi-Press Hardware Panic:** Triple-pressing the power button triggers the alarm without unlocking.*  
*2. **Background Acoustic Decibel Spike:** On-device lightweight models detect screams (>90 dB) and sudden fall impacts.*  
*3. **Voice Duress Keywords:** Spoken distress phrases ('Help') or subtle stealth codes ('The tea is getting cold') activate silent emergency protocols."*

### Q3: "What makes the payment distribution tamper-proof?"
**Answer:**  
*"Every settlement is computed using a deterministic mathematical formula (Gross minus 35% raw material reserve and 5% youth fee). The exact payout amount, member ID, order ID, and timestamp are hashed into a **SHA-256 cryptographic digest**. This hash is delivered via SMS and digital slip to each member, making it mathematically impossible for any group leader to alter individual shares."*
